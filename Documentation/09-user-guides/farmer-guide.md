# AgriConnect Ethiopia — Farmer Guide

## 1. Introduction

The AgriConnect Ethiopia platform helps farmers connect with buyers, agricultural experts, and logistics providers.

Farmers can use the platform to:

* Create and manage agricultural product listings.
* Make products available to buyers.
* Receive and manage orders.
* Request agricultural advice.
* Track delivery activities.
* Receive notifications.
* Use available AI-powered agricultural services.
* Manage their profile and account information.

---

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Create an Account

1. Open the AgriConnect application.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Enter your required personal information.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. Create a secure password.
6. Select **Farmer** as your user role.
7. Submit the registration form.
8. Complete any required account verification.

After successful registration, log in using your credentials.

---

## 3. Logging In

1. Open the AgriConnect login page.
2. Enter your registered email or username.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Login**.

After successful authentication, the system displays the farmer dashboard.

If the credentials are incorrect, verify the information and try again.

---

## 4. Farmer Dashboard

The farmer dashboard provides access to the main farmer features.

Typical dashboard functions include:

* My Products
* Orders
* Expert Services
* Notifications
* AI Services
* Profile
* Account Settings

The dashboard provides an overview of the farmer's activities.

---

# 5. Managing Products

## 5.1 Create a Product

To create a product listing:

1. Open **My Products**.
2. Select **Add Product**.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Select the product category.
5. Enter a description.
6. Enter the available quantity.
7. Enter the selling price.
8. Enter the location.
9. Add an image if supported.
10. Select **Save** or **Publish**.

The product becomes available in the marketplace after successful creation and publication.

---

## 5.2 Update a Product

To update an existing product:

1. Open **My Products**.
2. Select the product.
3. Select **Edit**.
4. Update the required information.
5. Save the changes.

The updated information is reflected in the product listing.

---

## 5.3 Remove a Product

To remove a product:

1. Open **My Products**.
2. Select the product.
3. Select **Delete**, **Remove**, or the applicable action.
4. Confirm the operation.

The product should no longer be available for new purchases when successfully removed or deactivated.

---

# 6. Managing Orders

Farmers can view orders placed by buyers.

To view orders:

1. Open the farmer dashboard.
2. Select **Orders**.
3. Select an order to view its details.

Order information may include:

* Order number
* Buyer
* Product
* Quantity
* Price
* Order date
* Delivery information
* Order status

---

## 6.1 Order Status

Order statuses may include:

* Pending
* Confirmed
* Processing
* Shipped
* Delivered
* Cancelled

The exact statuses depend on the implemented AgriConnect workflow.

Farmers should regularly check their orders for updates.

---

# 7. Expert Services

Farmers can request assistance from agricultural experts.

## 7.1 Request Expert Advice

1. Open **Expert Services**.
2. Browse available experts.
3. Select an appropriate expert.
4. Describe the agricultural problem.
5. Provide relevant information.
6. Submit the consultation request.

The selected expert can review the request and provide advice.

---

## 7.2 View Consultation Status

Farmers can monitor their consultation requests.

Possible statuses include:

* Pending
* Assigned
* In Progress
* Responded
* Completed
* Cancelled

---

# 8. AI Agricultural Services

If the AI service is enabled, farmers can use it to obtain agricultural recommendations.

To use the service:

1. Open **AI Services**.
2. Enter the required agricultural information.
3. Provide details about the crop, problem, or farming situation.
4. Submit the request.
5. Review the generated recommendation.

AI recommendations should be treated as decision-support information and should not replace professional agricultural advice when expert assistance is required.

---

# 9. Delivery and Logistics

Farmers can view delivery information associated with their orders.

Delivery information may include:

* Pickup location
* Destination
* Product
* Quantity
* Logistics provider
* Delivery status
* Expected delivery information

Farmers should ensure that pickup and destination information is accurate.

---

# 10. Notifications

The notification system can provide updates about:

* New orders
* Order status changes
* Delivery updates
* Expert responses
* Platform announcements
* Other important activities

To view notifications:

1. Open the notification icon or notification page.
2. Select a notification.
3. Review the information.
4. Mark it as read if the feature is available.

---

# 11. Profile Management

Farmers should keep their profile information up to date.

Profile information may include:

* Full name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Location
* Farm information
* Profile image
* Other supported information

To update the profile:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Select **Edit Profile**.
3. Update the required information.
4. Save the changes.

---

# 12. Account Security

Farmers should follow these security practices:

* Use a strong password.
* Never share account credentials.
* Log out on shared computers.
* Keep contact information updated.
* Avoid suspicious links.
* Report suspicious account activity.
* Do not share authentication tokens or passwords.

---

# 13. Troubleshooting

## Cannot Log In

Check:

* Email or username.
* Password.
* Internet connection.
* Account status.

If the problem continues, use the available password recovery or support process.

---

## Product Cannot Be Created

Check:

* Required fields.
* Product quantity.
* Product price.
* Category.
* Internet connection.

Correct the invalid information and try again.

---

## Product Is Not Visible

Check:

* Product publication status.
* Product availability.
* Product category.
* Search filters.
* Internet connection.

---

## Order Information Is Not Updated

Refresh the page and check the order again.

If the problem continues, record the order number and contact platform support.

---

## Expert Request Is Not Responded To

Check the consultation status and notification area.

If the request remains pending for an extended period, contact the platform administrator or support team.

---

# 14. Good Practices for Farmers

Farmers should:

* Provide accurate product information.
* Keep product quantities updated.
* Use realistic prices.
* Provide accurate locations.
* Respond to buyer orders promptly.
* Keep delivery information accurate.
* Use agricultural expert services when necessary.
* Keep their account secure.
* Check notifications regularly.

---

# 15. Support

When reporting a problem, provide:

* Your account role.
* Description of the problem.
* Date and time of the problem.
* Relevant product or order ID.
* Screenshot if available.
* Error message if displayed.

This information helps the support team investigate the problem efficiently.

---

# 16. Summary

The AgriConnect farmer interface provides farmers with tools to manage products, interact with buyers, request expert assistance, track orders and deliveries, receive notifications, and access agricultural decision-support services.

Farmers should keep their information accurate and use the platform responsibly to obtain the best results.

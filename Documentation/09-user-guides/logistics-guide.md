# AgriConnect Ethiopia — Logistics Guide

## 1. Introduction

The AgriConnect Ethiopia logistics functionality connects orders with logistics providers to support the movement of agricultural products from sellers to buyers.

Logistics providers can use the platform to:

* View delivery requests.
* Review delivery details.
* Accept delivery assignments.
* Update delivery status.
* Track assigned deliveries.
* Complete deliveries.
* Receive notifications.
* Manage their logistics profile.

---

# 2. Getting Started

## 2.1 Logistics Account

A logistics provider must have an approved logistics account before accessing logistics functionality.

Depending on the implemented platform workflow, account verification may be required.

After approval, the logistics provider can log in and access the logistics dashboard.

---

# 3. Logging In

1. Open the AgriConnect application.
2. Enter the registered email or username.
3. Enter the password.
4. Select **Login**.

After successful authentication, the logistics dashboard is displayed.

If login fails, verify the credentials and try again.

---

# 4. Logistics Dashboard

The logistics dashboard provides access to delivery-related functionality.

Typical features include:

* Delivery Requests
* Assigned Deliveries
* Active Deliveries
* Completed Deliveries
* Notifications
* Profile
* Account Settings

The dashboard may also display delivery statistics.

---

# 5. Managing the Logistics Profile

Logistics providers should maintain accurate profile information.

Information may include:

* Business or provider name.
* Contact person.
* Phone number.
* Email address.
* Operating location.
* Service areas.
* Available transportation types.
* Other supported information.

To update the profile:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Select **Edit Profile**.
3. Update the required information.
4. Save the changes.

---

# 6. Viewing Delivery Requests

To view available delivery requests:

1. Open **Delivery Requests**.
2. Review the available requests.
3. Select a request to view details.

A delivery request may contain:

* Delivery ID.
* Order ID.
* Pickup location.
* Destination.
* Product information.
* Quantity.
* Buyer information.
* Seller information.
* Requested date.
* Delivery status.

---

# 7. Reviewing a Delivery Request

Before accepting a delivery, review:

* Pickup location.
* Destination.
* Product quantity.
* Delivery requirements.
* Requested delivery time.
* Available transportation requirements.

Make sure the delivery can be completed before accepting it.

---

# 8. Accepting a Delivery

If the delivery is suitable:

1. Open the delivery request.
2. Review the details.
3. Select **Accept Delivery**.
4. Confirm the assignment.

The delivery should then appear under assigned or active deliveries.

Typical workflow:

```text
Available
    ↓
Accepted
    ↓
Pickup
    ↓
In Transit
    ↓
Delivered
```

The exact statuses depend on the implemented system.

---

# 9. Updating Delivery Status

Logistics providers should update delivery status as the delivery progresses.

Possible statuses include:

* Pending
* Accepted
* Pickup
* In Transit
* Delivered
* Cancelled

Example:

```text
Accepted
   ↓
Pickup Completed
   ↓
In Transit
   ↓
Delivered
```

Status updates allow sellers and buyers to monitor the delivery.

---

# 10. Pickup

When collecting the agricultural products:

1. Navigate to the pickup location.
2. Verify the order or delivery information.
3. Confirm the products and quantity.
4. Collect the products.
5. Update the delivery status.

The provider should report any discrepancy before continuing the delivery.

---

# 11. Delivery in Transit

After collecting the products:

1. Confirm that the products are secured.
2. Begin transportation.
3. Update the status to **In Transit**.
4. Follow the agreed delivery route.
5. Keep delivery information updated where tracking is supported.

---

# 12. Completing a Delivery

When the products reach the destination:

1. Confirm the delivery location.
2. Verify the order information.
3. Hand over the products to the intended recipient.
4. Confirm successful delivery.
5. Update the status to **Delivered**.

Additional proof of delivery may be required if supported by the platform.

---

# 13. Failed Delivery

If delivery cannot be completed:

1. Record the reason.
2. Update the delivery status if supported.
3. Notify the relevant parties.
4. Follow the platform's failed-delivery procedure.

Possible reasons include:

* Recipient unavailable.
* Incorrect destination.
* Vehicle problem.
* Road/access problem.
* Product issue.
* Other unexpected circumstances.

---

# 14. Delivery Cancellation

If cancellation is supported:

1. Open the assigned delivery.
2. Select the cancellation option.
3. Provide the reason.
4. Confirm cancellation.

Cancellation should only be performed according to the platform's rules and policies.

---

# 15. Notifications

Logistics providers may receive notifications for:

* New delivery requests.
* Delivery assignments.
* Order changes.
* Delivery status changes.
* Cancellation requests.
* Platform announcements.

Check notifications regularly to avoid missing important delivery updates.

---

# 16. Security and Privacy

Logistics providers may have access to sensitive delivery information.

They should:

* Keep login credentials private.
* Protect customer information.
* Avoid sharing delivery details unnecessarily.
* Log out from shared devices.
* Avoid suspicious links.
* Never share authentication tokens.
* Report suspicious account activity.

Only authorized delivery information should be accessed.

---

# 17. Troubleshooting

## No Delivery Requests

Check:

* Delivery filters.
* Service location.
* Delivery status.
* Account approval.
* Internet connection.

---

## Cannot Accept Delivery

Check:

* Delivery status.
* Account permissions.
* Availability.
* Internet connection.
* Displayed error message.

---

## Cannot Update Delivery Status

Check:

* Current delivery state.
* Required fields.
* Network connection.
* Account authorization.

Correct any validation errors and try again.

---

## Delivery Information Is Incorrect

Do not proceed based on uncertain information.

Verify the order and delivery details and contact the appropriate seller, buyer, or platform administrator.

---

## Notification Not Received

Check:

* Notification page.
* Account status.
* Internet connection.
* Notification settings.

If the problem continues, report it to platform support.

---

```text
  ↓
Review Delivery
  ↓
Accept Delivery
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

```

---

# 19. Good Practices

Logistics providers should:

* Verify pickup information.
* Confirm product quantity.
* Protect agricultural products during transportation.
* Keep delivery statuses updated.
* Communicate problems promptly.
* Protect buyer and seller information.
* Follow agreed delivery procedures.
* Confirm successful delivery.
* Maintain accurate profile information.

---

# 20. Support

When reporting a logistics problem, provide:

* Delivery ID.
* Order ID.
* Description of the issue.
* Pickup location.
* Destination.
* Date and time.
* Error message if applicable.
* Screenshot when available.

Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

---

# 21. Summary

The AgriConnect logistics functionality provides logistics providers with tools to manage delivery requests, accept assignments, update delivery status, coordinate product transportation, and confirm successful deliveries.

Accurate delivery information, timely status updates, responsible handling of products, and protection of customer information are essential for reliable logistics operations.

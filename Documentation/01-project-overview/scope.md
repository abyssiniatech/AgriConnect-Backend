# Project Scope

## 1. Scope Overview

AgriConnect Ethiopia is a digital agriculture platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, agricultural experts, logistics providers, and administrators through an integrated web-based ecosystem.

The project covers the analysis, design, development, testing, documentation, and deployment preparation of the platform and its core agricultural services.

The scope is divided into functional, technical, user, and operational areas.

---

## 2. In-Scope Features

### 2.1 User Management

The system will provide user management functionality for different stakeholder groups, including:

* Farmer registration and profile management.
* Buyer registration and profile management.
* Agricultural expert profiles.
* Logistics provider profiles.
* Administrator accounts.
* User authentication.
* User authorization.
* Role-based access control.
* Profile management.
* Account status management.

---

### 2.2 Farmer Management

Farmers will be able to:

* Register on the platform.
* Create and update their profiles.
* Manage agricultural products.
* Publish products to the marketplace.
* View product-related activities.
* Communicate with buyers.
* Request agricultural consultations.
* Request logistics services.
* Receive notifications.
* Access supported agricultural information and AI-assisted services.

---

### 2.3 Buyer Management

Buyers will be able to:

* Register and manage their profiles.
* Browse agricultural products.
* Search and filter products.
* View product details.
* View farmer information where permitted.
* Contact farmers.
* Submit purchase-related requests.
* Request logistics services.
* Receive relevant notifications.

---

### 2.4 Agricultural Expert Services

The platform will support agricultural expert services including:

* Expert registration.
* Expert profile management.
* Expertise and specialization information.
* Consultation requests.
* Communication between farmers and experts.
* Consultation management.
* Agricultural recommendations and guidance.

---

### 2.5 Logistics Management

The logistics module will support:

* Logistics provider registration.
* Transportation service management.
* Transportation requests.
* Delivery coordination.
* Communication between stakeholders.
* Logistics status management.
* Delivery-related notifications.

---

### 2.6 Marketplace

The marketplace will provide functionality for:

* Agricultural product listings.
* Product information.
* Product categories.
* Product search.
* Product filtering.
* Product availability.
* Farmer-product relationships.
* Buyer-product discovery.
* Purchase-related workflows.

The marketplace will serve as a central point for connecting agricultural producers and potential buyers.

---

### 2.7 AI-Assisted Agricultural Services

The platform may provide AI-assisted functionality to support agricultural decision-making.

Potential capabilities include:

* Agricultural question assistance.
* General crop-related guidance.
* Agricultural recommendations.
* Knowledge-based agricultural assistance.
* Intelligent information retrieval.
* Future AI-powered agricultural analysis.

AI functionality will be designed as an assistive service and should not replace qualified agricultural professionals where professional judgment is required.

---

### 2.8 Notification System

The system will support notifications for important platform activities, such as:

* Registration events.
* Marketplace activities.
* Purchase-related events.
* Consultation requests.
* Logistics requests.
* Delivery updates.
* Administrative notifications.
* Other important system events.

---

### 2.9 Administration

Administrators will have capabilities for:

* User management.
* Role and permission management.
* Platform monitoring.
* Marketplace monitoring.
* Content management.
* Report management.
* System activity monitoring.
* Administrative configuration.

## 7. Out-of-Scope Items

The following items are outside the initial scope of the project unless explicitly added during future development:

* Physical agricultural equipment management.
* Direct agricultural financing.
* Direct insurance services.
* Government policy management.
* Physical warehouse management.
* Ownership and operation of transportation vehicles.
* Guaranteed agricultural price prediction.
* Fully autonomous agricultural decision-making.
* Replacement of professional agricultural experts.
* Large-scale IoT hardware deployment.
* Agricultural drone management.
* Direct control of farm machinery.

These areas may be considered as future improvements or external integrations.

---

## 8. Scope Boundaries

AgriConnect Ethiopia is primarily a **digital platform**.

The project provides software-based services that facilitate communication, information exchange, marketplace activities, expert assistance, and logistics coordination.

The platform itself does not directly own agricultural products, operate farms, own transportation fleets, or provide professional agricultural guarantees.

Instead, it provides the digital infrastructure that enables stakeholders to interact more efficiently.

## 10. Scope Summary

The initial scope of AgriConnect Ethiopia focuses on creating a secure and scalable digital ecosystem connecting farmers, buyers, agricultural experts, logistics providers, and administrators.

The platform will provide user management, marketplace functionality, expert services, logistics coordination, notifications, AI-assisted agricultural support, security, APIs, testing, documentation, and deployment preparation.

The architecture and documentation will be designed to allow the platform to evolve as additional agricultural services and integrations are introduced in future versions.

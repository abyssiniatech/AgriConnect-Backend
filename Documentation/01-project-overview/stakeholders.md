# Stakeholders

## 1. Stakeholder Overview

AgriConnect Ethiopia involves multiple stakeholders who interact with the platform directly or are affected by its services.

The main stakeholders are:

1. Farmers
2. Buyers
3. Agricultural Experts
4. Logistics Providers
5. Administrators
6. System Developers
7. Project Owner / Management
8. Future Partners and External Service Providers

Each stakeholder has different responsibilities, needs, permissions, and expectations from the platform.

---

## 2. Farmers

### Description

Farmers are one of the primary users of AgriConnect Ethiopia. They use the platform to access markets, publish agricultural products, communicate with buyers, request expert assistance, and coordinate logistics.

### Main Needs

* Easy registration and profile management.
* Access to potential buyers.
* Ability to publish agricultural products.
* Product management.
* Access to agricultural experts.
* Logistics coordination.
* Agricultural information.
* Notifications and updates.
* AI-assisted agricultural support.

### Main Activities

Farmers can:

* Register and authenticate.
* Manage their profiles.
* Create agricultural product listings.
* Update product information.
* View marketplace activities.
* Communicate with buyers.
* Request expert consultations.
* Request logistics services.
* Receive notifications.

### Importance

**Critical**

Farmers are a core stakeholder group and one of the primary beneficiaries of the platform.

---

## 3. Buyers

### Description

Buyers use AgriConnect Ethiopia to discover agricultural products and connect with farmers.

### Main Needs

* Easy product discovery.
* Product search and filtering.
* Reliable product information.
* Farmer information.
* Direct communication with farmers.
* Purchase-related requests.
* Delivery coordination.
* Notifications.

### Main Activities

Buyers can:

* Register and authenticate.
* Manage their profiles.
* Browse products.
* Search and filter marketplace listings.
* View product details.
* Communicate with farmers.
* Submit purchase-related requests.
* Request logistics services.

### Importance

**Critical**

Buyers are essential to the marketplace ecosystem and help create market opportunities for farmers.

---

## 4. Agricultural Experts

### Description

Agricultural experts provide professional knowledge and guidance to farmers through the platform.

### Main Needs

* Professional profile.
* Expertise and specialization management.
* Consultation request management.
* Communication tools.
* Ability to provide agricultural guidance.
* Consultation history.

### Main Activities

Experts can:

* Register and manage profiles.
* Specify areas of expertise.
* Receive consultation requests.
* Communicate with farmers.
* Provide agricultural recommendations.
* Manage consultation activities.

### Importance

**High**

Experts provide knowledge-based services that can support farmers' agricultural decisions.

---

## 5. Logistics Providers

### Description

Logistics providers support the transportation and delivery of agricultural products.

### Main Needs

* Logistics provider profile.
* Transportation service management.
* Transportation requests.
* Delivery coordination.
* Communication with farmers and buyers.
* Delivery status management.

### Main Activities

Logistics providers can:

* Register and manage profiles.
* Publish or manage available transportation services.
* Receive transportation requests.
* Accept or manage delivery assignments.
* Coordinate with relevant stakeholders.
* Update logistics status.

### Importance

**High**

Efficient logistics is important for moving agricultural products between producers and buyers.

---

## 6. Administrators

### Description

Administrators are responsible for managing and monitoring the platform.

### Main Needs

* Administrative dashboard.
* User management.
* Role and permission management.
* Marketplace monitoring.
* Content management.
* System monitoring.
* Reports and administrative controls.

### Main Activities

Administrators can:

* Manage users.
* Manage roles and permissions.
* Monitor platform activities.
* Manage platform content.
* Review reports.
* Manage problematic accounts or content.
* Monitor system operations.

### Importance

**Critical**

Administrators ensure the platform operates securely, reliably, and according to established rules.

---

## 7. System Developers

### Description

Developers design, build, test, maintain, and improve the AgriConnect Ethiopia platform.

### Responsibilities

Developers are responsible for:

* Software architecture.
* Frontend development.
* Backend development.
* Database development.
* API development.
* Security implementation.
* Testing.
* Bug fixing.
* Performance optimization.
* Documentation.
* Deployment support.
* Future feature development.

### Importance

**Critical**

Developers maintain the technical foundation of the platform.

---

## 8. Project Owner / Management

### Description

The project owner or management team defines the business direction and evaluates whether the project meets its intended objectives.

### Responsibilities

Management may be responsible for:

* Defining project goals.
* Approving requirements.
* Reviewing project progress.
* Evaluating project outcomes.
* Prioritizing future improvements.
* Managing project resources.
* Reviewing risks and opportunities.

### Importance

**High**

Management provides strategic direction and ensures that the platform delivers meaningful value.

## 13. Stakeholder Expectations

All stakeholders expect the platform to provide:

* Reliability.
* Security.
* Usability.
* Transparency.
* Availability.
* Accurate information.
* Efficient communication.
* Appropriate privacy protection.
* Consistent system behavior.
* Useful agricultural services.

---

## 14. Stakeholder Success

AgriConnect Ethiopia will be considered successful from a stakeholder perspective when:

* Farmers can effectively access agricultural markets and services.
* Buyers can efficiently discover agricultural products.
* Experts can provide useful agricultural guidance.
* Logistics providers can efficiently coordinate transportation.
* Administrators can manage the platform effectively.
* Developers can maintain and extend the system.
* Management can evaluate measurable project outcomes.
* Future partners can integrate with the platform where appropriate.

---

## 15. Summary

AgriConnect Ethiopia is designed around a multi-stakeholder ecosystem.

Farmers and buyers form the core marketplace relationship, while agricultural experts provide knowledge-based services and logistics providers support transportation. Administrators maintain platform governance, developers maintain the technical system, and management provides strategic direction.

This stakeholder model establishes the foundation for defining detailed requirements, permissions, workflows, use cases, and acceptance criteria in the following sections of the project documentation.
